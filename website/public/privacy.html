Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ant Colony Colors ("we", "us") handles information when you use the Ant Colony Colors mobile game (the "App"). We designed the App to collect as little information as possible.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location. Your game progress, coins and settings are stored only on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, including app-open, interstitial and optional rewarded video ads. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, device type, operating system, app interactions with ads, and approximate location derived from your IP address.

On iOS, the App asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can change your choice at any time from Privacy Options in the App's Settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. How information is used

  • To show, limit the frequency of, and measure ads.
  • To detect and prevent fraud and invalid ad traffic.
  • To keep the App working and improve it (for example, crash and performance information collected by the advertising SDK).

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for any purpose other than those listed here.

4. Sharing

Information described in section 2 is shared with Google as our advertising provider and with the ad networks it works with. We do not share information with anyone else, except if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the App. We do not keep a copy. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, most requests are handled through the App's Privacy Options, your device settings, or directly with Google. You can always contact us for help.

8. Security

We rely on the security of iOS and of our advertising provider. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
