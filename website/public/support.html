Frequently asked questions

How do I play? Tap the first ant in a lane. It moves to a free nest and its ants eat the matching tiles on the outside of the picture. Clear every tile to win.

I am stuck. What can I do? Use Shuffle to reorder the waiting colonies, add an extra nest, or restart the level.

How do I unlock a picture early? In the Picture Collection, tap a locked picture and watch a short video to unlock it.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app? Progress is stored only on your device, so deleting the app also deletes your progress.

How do I change ad privacy choices? Open Settings in the app and choose Privacy Options (shown where required). You can also manage tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

How do I delete my data? We do not keep an account or personal data on our servers. Deleting the app removes all game data from your device. For data held by our advertising partner, see our Privacy Policy.
