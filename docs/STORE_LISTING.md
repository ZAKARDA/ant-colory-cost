## Description
Welcome to the colony. Ant Colony Colors is a relaxing color puzzle where you send colonies of ants to clear a pixel picture, one color at a time.

- Tap a colony to send it to a free nest. Its ants eat the matching tiles on the edge of the picture.
- Think ahead: outer layers hide the colors underneath, and your nests are limited.
- Finish colonies back-to-back for combo coin bonuses, shuffle the queue when you are stuck, and play a new Daily Puzzle every day.
- 150 original pictures, each one proven solvable.
- Soft glass-and-gold design, gentle music, no timers, no pressure.

Free to play with ads. Watching a video is always optional and can unlock an extra nest or a picture early.
